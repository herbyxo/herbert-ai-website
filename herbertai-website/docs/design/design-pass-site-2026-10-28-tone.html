The site’s tone of wording: three ways

The same eight lines (How it works) written in three tones. The one you pick becomes the rule for every line on the site, and the rest of /pilot gets rewritten to it. Facts are the pilot run sheet’s. 1440 left, 375 right.

TodayMixed: some plain facts, an aside (say an AI document chaser), a not-this-but-that line.
A. Plain factsSay what happens, in the plain words a buyer could check: short statements of fact, the same grammar on every line, no asides, no slogans, no not-this-but-that. The business speaks; steps say what happens. Recommended: it is how the lines you kept already read (One call on how your business runs, Your written report, No obligation).
B. The owner’s voiceHow an owner would put it to another owner: you-statements, contractions, conversational.
C. Spec sheetTerse noun phrases, like a product’s spec list: the fewest words that name the thing.
