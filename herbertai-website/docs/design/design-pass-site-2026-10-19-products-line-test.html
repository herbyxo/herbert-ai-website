Products: the line test

Every candidate that passed the gates (true today or flagged, no promised result, no ad platform named), scored and sorted. Mark the numbers that grab you, and the ones that do not; each pick teaches the next round more than another three options would.

Scale: 0 to 5, one point each, half for partly. Picture, Prove and Only us are Harry Dry’s three questions (can I picture it, could it be proved wrong, could nobody else say it), which Hopkins, Ogilvy and Julian Shapiro back. Device: one structural move (a pair, a three, a twisted phrase, a mirror, a kicker), from the swipe file, where praised lines stack two moves and flat feature rows carry one or none. Your shape: short, one claim, no qualifier tail, from the lines you picked in the ledger over my recommendations. Gates from the memorability studies too: a word you can picture, the first two words carry the row, no hype words, and the line still reads plainly if the pun is missed.
