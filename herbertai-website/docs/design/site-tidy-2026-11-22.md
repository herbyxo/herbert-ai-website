## Recommended

Keep, nine page files, six of them pages a visitor sees:

- `/`, `/pilot` (with `/pilot/sample-audit` and `/pilot/thanks`), `/faq`: the three Will named.
- `/pricing`: the price is the first thing an owner looks for, the home page and /pilot section 5 both point at it, and four prices do not fit inside /pilot.
- `/about`: visitors arrive from outreach to check the person is real, and the home page's founder note links to it. The email address from /contact moves here.
- `/privacy`: the forms take personal details.
- `/demo/[industry]`: not a site page; every cold email links to it.

Remove, each with a redirect:

- `/services` to `/`: Will named it; the home page's AI Employees and Operating Systems sections show the same things.
- `/industries` and its four pages to `/`: Will named it; the home page's Operating Systems section has a screen per industry. The five new industry pages the charter planned are dropped with it.
- `/work` and its two example pages to `/`: reached from the footer only, no search impressions, and two of its six entries describe engagements that ended.
- `/start` and `/contact` to `/pilot#book`: the audit form is the one way in. `/start/thanks` goes once nothing lands on it.
- `/ai-receptionist`, `/web-design-adelaide`, `/google-ads-adelaide` and the three `/lp/*` pages to `/`: they sell web design, Google Ads management and a receptionist, which the ladder no longer sells, and none had a search impression in three months.
- `/lab`: when the rebuild ships, as already planned. Not now; section 7 still needs it.

The header becomes How it works, Pricing, About.

Two things removing the ads pages costs: the paused Google Ads groups lose their destinations, and Herbert Reach loses the control page and landing pages its own test uses (`code/marketing-engine`, 16 references to /web-design-adelaide). Both are off today; restarting either means rebuilding a landing page.
