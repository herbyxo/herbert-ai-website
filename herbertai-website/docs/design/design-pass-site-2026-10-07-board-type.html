Hero detail 6: the board’s type and colours

Each option changes one thing about the board. The board is shown large on the left and in place on the right.

TodayMono for the header and times, employee name in black, job in dark grey, status words in green or amber. For comparison, not an option.
1. No monoThe header and the times move from the typewriter-style mono into the site’s own font. Quieter and more like a finished product; loses a little of the ’software log’ feel.
2. The job firstThe job text goes black and medium weight and the employee’s name goes grey, so the eye lands on what happened, not on who did it.
3. Colour only on the lampThe status words go grey and only the dot carries the colour, so the board reads black and white with green lamps. Closest to the charter’s rule that colour means state and nothing else. Recommended.
