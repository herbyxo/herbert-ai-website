How it works, detail 5: spacing

The screen a visitor sees on a 1440 by 900 laptop after scrolling to the section, clean on the left and measured on the right: above the label, heading to steps, steps to link, below the link.

Today129 / 64 / 66 / 131px, 828px tall. For comparison, not an option.
1. Tighter97 / 48 / 50 / 99px, 732px tall. The section is three short lists now, so it reads as one compact block and the next section shows at the bottom of the screen.
2. More air161 / 80 / 66 / 163px, 908px tall. Calmer and more spaced out, at a fixed size: on a 900px-tall laptop it looks almost the same as 3, but on a shorter screen it runs on past the fold and on a taller one the next section shows.
3. One screen165 / 64 / 66 / 167px, 900px tall. Like the hero you picked: the section fills whatever screen it is on with its content centred, so each scroll stop lands on one whole section. Recommended, for one rhythm down the page; on a large monitor the space above and below grows with the screen.
