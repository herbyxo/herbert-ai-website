Hero detail 7: spacing

The whole first screen of a 1440 by 900 laptop, so the fold (the bottom edge) shows. Clean on the left; on the right the three gaps are measured: above the headline, between the text and the board, and below the hero.

Today80px above, 48px between, 115px below; 143px of the next section shows above the fold. For comparison, not an option.
1. TighterLess space above, between and below. More of the next section shows above the fold (207px of it), and the cut-off heading at the fold pulls people into scrolling. Recommended.
2. More airMore space above and below; the gap between the text and the board stays, because any wider and “More money.” breaks onto two lines. Calmer and more premium, but on a laptop only 79px of the next section shows, so little hints that the page keeps going.
3. Exactly one screenThe hero fills the screen under the header with the content centred in it, and the next section starts right at the fold. Clean, but nothing below hints that the page keeps going.
