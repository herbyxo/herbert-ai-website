# The cold read (30 Oct 2026)

Will, 30 Oct 2026, after onboarding Liam: "a lot of the information on the site
doesnt really make sense? if i was someone who had no idea about the business,
some of the points are hard to get an idea of what it actually is". Every line
on the organic pages read as an owner who has never heard of Herbert AI, landing
from a search or a salesperson's mention. A line is listed when it only works
with knowledge from inside the business. Beside each one: what that reader
meets, and a plain version. Nothing here is built; Will marks the rows.

How to mark: "all" builds every plain version; "all except 7, 12" leaves those
two as they are; a row number with your own words replaces mine.

The pattern across the site: the plain definitions exist. /faq's first answer
("software that does one office job every day, like chasing documents,
answering enquiries or confirming bookings, with someone on your team approving
anything that goes out") and /services' second section say what the things are.
They sit at the bottom of the funnel, and the home page, /pilot and the ladder
use the terms bare. The fix is the same term said plainly the first time it
appears on each page, which is now rule 8 of the charter.
